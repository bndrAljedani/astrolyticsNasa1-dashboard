THE BIG QUESTION

What changes when fire leaves Earth?

Earth-based fire tests do not cover every oxygen, gravity, material and airflow combination proposed for Moon and Mars habitats. This platform makes the observed evidence—and the gaps—visible.

Evidence gaps.

CRITICAL SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS
GAP 01

Partial gravity

These CSVs do not provide direct Moon (1/6 g) or Mars (~0.38 g) combustion results. Earth and microgravity findings are bounds, not replacements.

Gravity · missing in source tables
GAP 02

Real materials at high O₂

High-oxygen droplets or gas flames do not establish flammability for cabin fabrics, plastics or spacecraft components under the same conditions.

Material transfer · not established
GAP 03

Airflow + outcome

No dataset in this archive varies oxygen, pressure and airflow while consistently recording outcomes. Three-dimensional predictions would be misleading.

01 / SOURCE MATERIAL

Original PSI CSV tables

The user-provided archive contains 22 experimental CSV files related to 20 PSI investigation IDs. Four additional ground-study investigations are documented in the project research guide but have no CSV in this upload.

↓ Normalized JSON data
02 / CONVERSION

Units and contextual integrity

FLEX mmHg was converted to psi by ×14.6959488/760. CFI-G bar was converted by ×14.5037738. Oxygen mole fraction was multiplied by 100. Inverse burner-stream oxygen was not merged with cabin ambient oxygen.

↓ Reproducible Python cleaning script
03 / VALIDATION

Recorded values, not invented outcomes

Blank padding and invalid map coordinates are excluded from analyses but retained in the audit. This dashboard performs programmatic checks, not independent human verification against each original NASA record.

↓ Data quality report
04 / INTERPRETATION

Rules, not simulated AI

Insights and rankings are explainable rules or editorial descriptions linked to PSI studies. This GitHub Pages deployment does not contain an LLM, prediction API or trained machine-learning model. No feature is mislabeled as a working AI system.
